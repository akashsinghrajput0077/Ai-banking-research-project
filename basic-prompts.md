# Basic Initial Prompts Used in Research

These are the initial, less specific prompts used at the beginning of the research process.

## Initial Exploration Prompts

1. "Tell me about AI in banking"
   - Used with: ChatGPT
   - Purpose: Get general overview of AI applications in banking sector
   - Date: Early research phase

2. "How is AI used in Indian banks?"
   - Used with: Claude
   - Purpose: Focus on Indian banking context specifically
   - Date: Early research phase

3. "AI applications in HDFC Bank"
   - Used with: Perplexity
   - Purpose: Find specific examples from a major Indian bank
   - Date: Early research phase

4. "Fraud detection using machine learning"
   - Used with: Gemini
   - Purpose: Understand technical aspects of fraud detection systems
   - Date: Early research phase

5. "AI chatbots in banking customer service"
   - Used with: All tools
   - Purpose: Explore customer service applications
   - Date: Early research phase

## Limitations of Basic Prompts Noted

These initial prompts produced responses that were:
- Too general and lacked specific examples
- Missing quantitative data and measurable impacts
- Often outdated (referring to 2020-2021 implementations)
- Lacked critical analysis of risks and challenges
- Didn't distinguish between different banks' approaches
- Provided superficial overview without depth
