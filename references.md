- Looking for corroboration between news reports and official disclosures
- Consulting academic sources for technical validation of ML approaches

## Limitations of Sources Acknowledged

1. **Selective Transparency:** Banks often share successes but may underreport challenges or failures in public documents
2. **Reporting Lag:** Annual reports reflect previous fiscal year; very recent innovations may be underrepresented
3. **Marketing Bias:** Bank blogs and press releases may emphasize positive outcomes
4. **Analyst Estimates:** Consulting firm reports sometimes rely on modeling and estimates rather than disclosed data
5. **Regulatory Grey Areas:** Some AI applications (like alternative credit scoring) operate in evolving regulatory frameworks where public information is limited
